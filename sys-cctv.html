CCTV & surveillance

IP camera deployment, NVR configuration and remote monitoring — specified around the coverage a site actually needs, not the number of cameras a quotation can carry.

HikvisionDahuaIP / PoENVR & DVRONVIFRemote monitoring

The situation

Surveillance is routinely sold by unit count. The result is a dozen cameras, overlapping sightlines, and a blind spot at the one entrance that mattered — paired with footage too low-resolution to identify anyone and a retention window that expired before the incident was reported.

How I approach it

Coverage is planned from the threat model backwards. I map the sightlines a site genuinely requires, specify sensor and lens to the identification distance at each position, and size storage against a retention period agreed in advance rather than whatever the recorder shipped with.

In practice

What the work covers.

Scope of work

  • Site assessment, sightline mapping and camera position specification
  • IP camera installation, lens selection and field-of-view calibration
  • NVR and DVR configuration, recording schedules and motion zones
  • Storage sizing against a defined retention period
  • Remote and mobile viewing over secured connections
  • Network isolation so camera traffic never competes with operational bandwidth

What it delivers

  • Footage of sufficient resolution to identify, not merely to detect
  • No blind spots at the positions that carry actual risk
  • Retention that still holds footage when an incident surfaces late
  • Remote visibility without exposing the recorder to the open internet
