Automation & scripting

Scripted tooling that retires repetitive systems work — the tasks quietly consuming hours a week that nobody has stopped to measure.

BashPythonCronsystemd timersGitAnsible

The situation

Manual routine accumulates invisibly. Reports assembled by hand, accounts provisioned one at a time, the same cleanup run every Monday — each small enough to tolerate, collectively a significant standing cost and a reliable source of human error.

How I approach it

I identify the tasks with the highest frequency-times-duration cost, script them, and make the result observable so failures surface rather than passing silently. Automation that nobody can see is automation nobody can trust.

In practice

What the work covers.

Scope of work

  • Audit of recurring manual tasks and their true time cost
  • Bash and Python tooling for provisioning, cleanup and reporting
  • Scheduled jobs with logging, failure alerting and safe retries
  • Bulk operations across fleets of machines or accounts
  • Data extraction and report generation on a fixed cadence
  • Documentation so the tooling outlives the person who wrote it

What it delivers

  • Hours returned each week to work that actually requires judgement
  • Consistent execution, removing the variance of manual repetition
  • Failures that announce themselves rather than passing unnoticed
  • Processes that survive handover intact
