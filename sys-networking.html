Networking

Structured cabling, routing, switching and segmented network design — built so throughput stays predictable and a fault in one segment never becomes an outage across the building.

The situation

Most small and mid-sized networks grow by accretion: a switch added here, an access point there, no addressing plan and no documentation. Performance degrades in ways nobody can trace, and a single misbehaving device can saturate a link for everyone.

How I approach it

I design the network as a deliberate topology rather than an accumulation of hardware — addressing scheme first, then segmentation, then physical layout. Every run is labelled and documented, so the next person to touch it is not reverse-engineering the cabinet.

In practice

What the work covers.

Scope of work

  • Site survey, cable routing and structured cabling to a documented standard
  • VLAN segmentation separating guest, operational and management traffic
  • Router and managed-switch configuration, including inter-VLAN routing rules
  • Wireless coverage planning, channel allocation and access-point placement
  • Firewall policy, port forwarding and secured remote access
  • Link monitoring and bandwidth analysis to isolate degradation early

What it delivers

  • Predictable throughput under concurrent load
  • Faults isolated to a segment instead of propagating network-wide
  • A documented topology that survives staff turnover
  • Guest and operational traffic that cannot reach one another
