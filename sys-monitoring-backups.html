Monitoring & backups

Alerting and backup regimes that surface problems before they become outages — and that are verified by restoration rather than assumed from a green status indicator.

ZabbixGrafanarsyncOffsite replicationCronUptimeRobot

The situation

Continuity planning is typically deferred until the first serious loss. Backups exist without a tested restore path, alerting is either absent or so noisy it gets ignored, and the true downtime estimate is discovered during the outage itself.

How I approach it

I start from the recovery objectives — how long the organisation can be down, how much data it can lose — and design the regime to satisfy them. Alerts are tuned to be actionable, because an alert nobody trusts is worse than none at all.

In practice

What the work covers.

Scope of work

  • Recovery point and recovery time objectives defined with stakeholders
  • Layered backup regimes spanning local, offsite and cloud targets
  • Automated verification and scheduled test restorations
  • Service and uptime monitoring with actionable alert thresholds
  • Log aggregation and retention for post-incident analysis
  • Incident response procedures documented and rehearsed

What it delivers

  • Backups that have been restored from, not merely reported as successful
  • Recovery objectives stated in numbers rather than intentions
  • Alerts that are acted on because they are trusted
  • Clear visibility of what is protected and what deliberately is not
