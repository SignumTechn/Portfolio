Project 04 · Web platform

Trailquest

A discovery and planning platform for road trips and hikes — built for adventure hunters who want real trail conditions, not scattered blog posts.

RoleFull-stack engineer
Timeline2026
StackNext.js · Node.js · Mapbox · PostgreSQL
trailquest.app/plan

The problem

Planning a hike or a road trip usually means piecing together trail difficulty, road conditions and points of interest from scattered blogs, forums and outdated reviews — with no single source anyone actually trusts.

The solution

Trailquest brings curated trails and road-trip routes, difficulty ratings, packing checklists and community trip reports into one map-first platform, so a rough idea becomes a plan someone can actually follow.

How it works

From idea to trailhead.

01

Discover

Adventurers browse trails and road-trip routes by region, difficulty and duration, filtered down to whatever fits the weekend they've got.

02

Plan the route

A route gets laid out on an interactive map, with elevation profile and estimated drive or hike time calculated before anyone leaves the house.

03

Pack & prepare

A packing checklist is generated automatically from trip length, terrain and season, so nothing critical gets left behind.

04

Log & share

After the trip, a report — photos, conditions, what changed on the ground — gets logged and feeds back into the platform for the next person planning the same route.
