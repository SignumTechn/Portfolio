Project 01 · Web platform

Kenya Car Intelligence

A research platform that helps car buyers in Kenya make an informed decision before they spend money — market prices, valuation, ownership and import costs, and buying guides in one place.

The problem

Buying a car in Kenya means piecing together information from dealer listings, forums and word of mouth — market prices, real ownership costs, and import duties all live in different places, if they're written down at all.

The solution

One platform that brings valuation, cost breakdowns and buying guides together, so a buyer can go from "which car" to "how much will this actually cost me" without leaving the site.

How it works

From research to decision.

01

Explore & compare

Buyers browse cars by make, model and price, or put two vehicles side by side to compare specs and estimated running costs directly.

02

Valuation & market prices

A valuation tool estimates a fair price for a specific vehicle using current market data, so a buyer knows if a listing is over- or under-priced before making an offer.

03

Ownership & import cost breakdowns

Separate calculators lay out what a car actually costs to run locally, and what it costs to bring in from abroad — duties, fees and all — turned into numbers instead of guesswork.

04

Guides & car finder

Buying guides answer the recurring questions — inspection checklists, petrol vs diesel, what to check before buying — while the car finder narrows the whole catalogue down to a shortlist that fits the buyer's budget.
