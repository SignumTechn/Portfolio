Service 07 · Systems, DevOps & support

Troubleshooting & support

End-user and escalation support structured around root cause — closing the ticket and closing the underlying issue are treated as two separate obligations.

WindowsLinuxRemote supportImagingTicketing systemsDocumentation

The situation

Support functions frequently optimise for closure rate. Tickets are resolved by restart and reissue, the same fault returns the following week, and nobody accumulates the knowledge that would prevent the third occurrence.

How I approach it

Every recurring ticket is treated as a defect in the environment rather than an inevitability. I resolve the immediate issue, then document the cause and address it at source — so routine ticket volume falls over time instead of holding steady.

In practice

What the work covers.

Scope of work

  • First- and second-line support across hardware, software and connectivity
  • Structured diagnosis and escalation paths with defined ownership
  • Workstation deployment, imaging and endpoint configuration
  • Knowledge-base authoring so recurring issues resolve without escalation
  • User training on the systems and tooling they depend on daily
  • Trend analysis across ticket history to identify systemic causes

What it delivers

  • Declining ticket volume as root causes are retired
  • Resolution paths that do not depend on one person being reachable
  • Users capable of resolving routine issues independently
  • Escalations that arrive with diagnosis already attached
